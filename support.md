## Common questions

**Why aren't my steps showing up?**
Go Go Crawler reads steps from Apple Health. Check that permission is granted in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Go Go Crawler. Steps are counted from the moment the app was installed.

**I bought the full game but the floors are locked.**
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases.

**How do I earn runs?**
Banked steps buy runs. You also earn them from workout quests on the Adventure Board and from achievements.

**How do I delete my account?**
